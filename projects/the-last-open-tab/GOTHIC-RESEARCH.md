## What the synthesis should actually look like

The tonal DNA is **gothic sets + pop-bright interruptions + dark comedy + a fair mystery**. It is not a collage of protected scenes. The active visual palette is ink-black, theatre burgundy, projector gold and one acidic neon accent. The ordinary enemy is a premature conclusion, embodied by the Presenter and increasingly by the city's habits. The action engine uses visible choices under a deadline: recover a missing page, block a false slide, change what the audience hears. Conflict stays human even when a door of light appears.

For Episode 001, add a flutter of papers when the projector loops, a deliberately overconfident “CONCLUSION” slide in celebratory neon, Ivo's absurdly earnest show-call panic, and a fast push-in on pages 26 and 28. The quiet blank wall after he turns off the slide should be the emotional counterweight. These are original staging decisions and do not reproduce any cited work's iconography.
