## Up to three-minute vertical play — beat sheet

**0–10:** repeated ending slide; Mara asks about page twenty-seven. **10–30:** the title projects as a question; Ivo's show deadline. **30–50:** the rule: disagreement opens a question door. **50–80:** pages twenty-six and twenty-eight, missing twenty-seven; June states what is known. **80–110:** projected image jumps; Ivo admits he saw a coat, not a face. **110–135:** mentor note falls from folder; authorship remains unverified. **135–160:** Ivo turns off the neat conclusion; Mara records the team decision. **160–175:** gold line remains; theatre opens. **175–180:** coat appears in hall, Mara says “We can check.”

2. Insert: page numbers 26 and 28 with a visible gap. No misleading fake document branding.
3. Mara single: she looks at the page, not at camera, when she says “Exactly.”
4. Wall insert: question projection; keep bright lettering legible on a phone.
5. June single: still, careful; her “unknown” lines are active choices.

7. Projection insert: original staged footage of mentor entering, then a hard missing frame. The cut must be visibly incomplete.

9. Remote closeup, then one breath of blank wall.
10. Floor closeup: gold line remains after the projected door closes.
11. Doorway silhouette and coat. End on Mara choosing to check.

Film at sufficient resolution for each composed frame. Protect highlights on projected text. Take room tone and a clean projector click. Prioritize intelligible dialogue over music. Make captions in one or two readable lines and verify their placement against app controls.

## Editor's rules

Use the repeated slide as pattern, not filler. The first visible contradiction appears by seven seconds in the teaser and by fifteen seconds in the long cut. The silent wall beat should feel like a choice. Avoid generic suspense music that pretends the factual question has a supernatural answer. If the 180-second cut feels rushed, release a shorter complete cut and keep the full play separate. Encode a 720p web preview with faststart and poster; load the player only when clicked. The existing local export helper in `/workspace/video-playbook/2026-10-04/export_video.py` can create broad-compatibility MP4 files. It has been smoke tested on a silent source; test this episode's audio and captions before release.

## Sound and access

Record dialogue close enough to understand on phone speakers. Capture one minute of room tone for edits. Create an SRT from the final audio, proof it manually against dialogue and speaker turns, then convert to VTT for the blog player. Include a transcript below the video. Use descriptive alt text for posters; do not put major plot information only in color. Avoid flickering projection effects. Original music or no music. Keep any sound cue below speech.
