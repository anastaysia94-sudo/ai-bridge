## Domain economics rule

Cheap first-year registration is not the same as cheap ownership.

Before every purchase, record:
- first-year price;
- ICANN/tax/checkout total;
- renewal price;
- purpose;
- whether we intend to renew;
- whether it will be primary, redirect, defensive hold, campaign domain, or disposable experiment.

## Domain hierarchy once funds improve

1. Founder Dynasty primary: `founderdynasty.com`.
2. Cashh Radar primary: `cashhradar.com`.
3. Corporate parent: `smartpickshopholdings.com`.
4. Only then consider project-specific domains for proven products with users/revenue.
5. Everything else should normally use subdomains of an owned primary domain or remain on a free platform until it earns the right to have another renewal bill.

## Lucrative-domain rule

Do not buy random cheap domains merely hoping to flip them. With a tiny budget, the highest expected value comes from exact names attached to products already being built. Domains become lucrative because they improve trust, conversion, discoverability, brand defensibility, email identity, and product independence — not because a $0.70 extension magically becomes valuable.
