5. Do not regenerate approved graphics when the task is to crop, resize, split, clean up, package, or otherwise work on the existing approved asset.
6. Preserve aliases and predecessor names. Do not silently create a duplicate product because an older and newer name look different.
7. Do not declare a product "finished" merely because source code or a build exists. Verify the actual requested artifact/runtime.
8. New project ideas, even one-line mentions, must be added here as **MENTION / IDEA SEED** rather than allowed to disappear.
9. `smartpickshop.dev` is **NOT OWNED**. It was an idea only. Do not use it in deployment instructions, DNS plans, branding, email, links, or architecture unless ownership is later explicitly verified.

# Known mistakes to prevent

1. **PTEDBoss drift:** rebuilding/regenerating a generic version instead of preserving the exact approved design and functionality.
2. **Graphics drift:** generating new graphics when the user asked to separate/crop/clean existing approved composite graphics.
3. **Status inflation:** calling source code, CI, a mock, or a generated file "finished" without requested runtime/artifact verification.
4. **Project amnesia:** forgetting old names, predecessor systems, physical projects, or one-line idea seeds.
5. **Duplicate-system sprawl:** creating a fresh CRM, lead engine, money app, or content engine without first reconciling overlaps.
6. **Domain invention:** assuming a suggested domain is owned.
7. **Hosting churn:** moving a free healthy deployment merely to make everything live on one provider.
8. **Repo-name inference:** assuming a repo's canonical purpose from its name instead of inspecting contents/workflows/history.
9. **Asset fabrication:** inventing a download link or claiming an old graphic/file exists when it is only referenced historically.
10. **Commercial-proof inflation:** do not claim customers, revenue, replies, payments, conversions, or results without evidence.
