# Trend Labs Intelligence Method — Canonical Working Spec

## Purpose
Use current evidence to identify commercially useful opportunities, not merely popular topics.

## Core distinctions
Always separate:
- facts
- estimates
- forecasts
- assumptions
- unknowns

Maintain an evidence ledger so every important conclusion can be traced to its source.

## Required inputs
- Current trend signals
- Evidence of buyer demand
- Evidence of urgency
- Competition and market saturation
- Pricing and willingness to pay
- Ease and speed of product creation
- Speed to market
- Recurring revenue potential
- Evidence confidence
- Profitability and revenue potential forecast

## Validation rules
- Validate each important trend using multiple independent current signals.
- Separate trend popularity from actual buyer demand.
- Find evidence of people and/or businesses actively experiencing the underlying problem and/or need.
- Check willingness to pay.
- Identify unmet needs, recurring complaints, workarounds, wasted time, lost money, and underserved buyer groups.
- Look for evidence that buyers already spend money on adjacent solutions.
- Compare competing products or services and record what buyers still dislike or cannot get.
- Distinguish crowded demand from underserved demand.
- Record contradictory evidence instead of hiding it.
- Do not treat one viral post, one review, or one search spike as proof of a market.

## Scoring dimensions
Score opportunities using:
1. Demand
2. Competition
3. Urgency
4. Monetization
5. Ease and speed to build
6. Reusability / recurring potential
7. Evidence confidence

Each score must include a plain-language explanation and source evidence.

## Opportunity discovery
After identifying a trend:
- Map the underlying problem.
- Identify who has the problem.
- Identify what they currently do instead.
- Identify where current solutions fail.
- Look between the trend and the obvious product for overlooked revenue angles.
- Surface non-obvious products, services, subscriptions, tooling, implementation, templates, data, education, licensing, or hybrid offers.
- Prioritize concrete buyer outcomes over novelty.

## Evidence ledger
For each important claim, record:
- claim
- evidence/source
- date
- signal type
- whether it is fact, estimate, forecast, assumption, or unknown
- confidence level
- contradictory evidence
- implication for the opportunity

## Output
For each opportunity provide:
- opportunity name
- target buyer
- problem/need
- evidence summary
- willingness-to-pay evidence
- competition summary
- unmet need
- proposed product/service angle
- demand score
- competition score
- urgency score
- monetization score
- build-speed/ease score
- recurring/reusability score
- evidence-confidence score
- overall opportunity score
- risks/unknowns
- next validation action

## Guardrails
- Never invent demand, customers, revenue, pricing, reviews, or sources.
- Do not confuse popularity with commercial intent.
- Mark forecasts as forecasts.
- Prefer multiple independent sources.
- If evidence is weak, say so.
